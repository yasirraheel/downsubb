Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

SubDown is designed to collect as little as possible. There is no account, no login, and no sign-up. This page explains what is processed when you use the Service.

1. What we process

  • Video links you paste. The link is sent to our server so it can fetch the video you asked for. It is used only for that download and is not sold or shared.
  • Downloaded files. A finished video is kept on our server only temporarily, so you can download it, and is then deleted automatically (currently within 24 hours). Unfinished downloads are also deleted automatically.
  • Technical logs. Like most websites, our server briefly keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and requested pages) for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting. Logs are not used to identify you personally and are not sold or shared with advertisers.

2. What stays on your own device

Your download history ("Your downloads") is stored in your own browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never sent to our server. Clearing your browser data removes it.

3. Cookies and tracking

SubDown does not use tracking cookies and does not sell personal data. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on the download page.

4. Third parties

When you paste a link, our server contacts the website that hosts the video (for example YouTube, TikTok, or Facebook) to fetch it, as you instructed. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and any data they collect is governed by them, not by us.

5. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the links you submit and the content you download. Downloading unauthorized content, or content that is blocked or restricted in your country, is your own responsibility. See our Terms of Service for the full rule.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Data security and retention

Downloaded files are deleted automatically as described above. Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, then removed.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy at any time. The updated version applies from the moment it is published on this page.
